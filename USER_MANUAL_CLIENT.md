## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the David's Salon homepage
2. Look for the **"Login"** or **"Client Portal"** link
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Enter your password
5. Click **"Sign In"**

**Forgot your password?**
1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Follow the recovery instructions sent to your email

### First-Time Registration

1. Click **"Register"** or **"Create Account"** on the homepage
2. Fill in your personal information:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number
   - Password (strong password recommended)
3. Accept the terms and conditions
4. Click **"Sign Up"**
5. Verify your email (if required)

## Booking Appointments

### How to Book an Appointment

1. Click **"Book Appointment"** from the dashboard or main menu
2. Select your preferred **Branch** (if multiple locations available)
3. Choose a **Service Category** (e.g., Haircut, Hair Coloring, Nail Care)
4. Select a specific **Service** from the category
5. Choose your preferred **Stylist** (or leave as "Any Stylist")
6. Select a **Date** - Calendar shows available dates in green
7. Select a **Time Slot** - Available times are highlighted
8. Review the service details:
   - Service duration
   - Service price
   - Stylist name
9. Click **"Confirm Booking"**

### Booking Confirmation

After booking:

- You'll see a **confirmation message** with your appointment details
- A **confirmation email** will be sent to your registered email
- Your appointment appears in **"My Appointments"**
- You'll receive **SMS/push notifications** reminders

### Booking Restrictions

- **Advance Booking:** Book up to 30 days in advance
- **Minimum Notice:** Appointments must be booked at least 2 hours in advance
- **Double-Booking Prevention:** You cannot book overlapping appointments with the same stylist
- **Branch Hours:** Only available time slots during branch operating hours are shown

## Managing Appointments

### View Your Appointments

1. Click **"My Appointments"** from the menu
2. Your appointments are displayed in chronological order
3. Filter by status: **Upcoming**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled**

### Appointment Status

- **Pending** - Awaiting confirmation
- **Confirmed** - Scheduled and confirmed
- **Completed** - Service finished
- **Cancelled** - Appointment cancelled

### Reschedule an Appointment

1. Go to **"My Appointments"**
2. Find the appointment you want to reschedule
3. Click **"Reschedule"** or the pencil icon
4. Select a new date and time
5. Confirm the new appointment details
6. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Rescheduling Rules:**
- Only pending or confirmed appointments can be rescheduled
- Must reschedule at least 2 hours before the appointment
- New slot must have same or similar service availability

### Cancel an Appointment

1. Go to **"My Appointments"**
2. Find the appointment to cancel
3. Click **"Cancel"** or the trash icon
4. Confirm the cancellation
5. You'll receive a cancellation confirmation email

**Cancellation Policy:**
- Cancel at least 2 hours in advance to avoid penalties
- Same-day cancellations may have fees (check your salon's policy)
- Cancellation reason is optional but helpful

### Appointment Details

When viewing an appointment, you can see:

- **Date & Time** - Scheduled appointment time
- **Duration** - How long the service takes
- **Stylist** - Your assigned stylist
- **Service** - What service you're getting
- **Price** - Service cost
- **Branch** - Location details and address
- **Status** - Current appointment status
- **Notes** - Any special requests or notes

## Promotions & Rewards

### Viewing Promotions

1. Click **"Promotions"** from the menu
2. See all current and upcoming promotional offers
3. Each promotion shows:
   - **Promotion Title**
   - **Description** - What's included
   - **Discount Amount** - How much you save
   - **Valid Until** - Expiration date
   - **Applicable Services** - Which services qualify
   - **Terms & Conditions** - Restrictions

### Applying Promotions

Promotions are **automatically applied** during billing if you qualify.

### Loyalty Rewards Program

#### Earning Points

- Earn loyalty points on every service purchase
- Points accumulate based on service amount
- View your **current point balance** on the dashboard

#### Checking Rewards

1. Click **"Rewards"** from the menu
2. View:
   - **Current Points** - Total accumulated points
   - **Points History** - How you earned/used points
   - **Redeemable Rewards** - What you can redeem
   - **Tier Status** - Your loyalty tier

#### Redeeming Rewards

1. Go to **"Rewards"**
2. Browse available rewards
3. Click **"Redeem"** on a reward
4. Confirm the redemption
5. Your points are deducted and reward is applied

**Reward Types:**
- Discount vouchers (percentage off)
- Free services
- Exclusive offers
- Birthday specials

## Profile Management

### Accessing Your Profile

1. Click on your **name/avatar** in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Profile"** or **"My Account"**

### Profile Information

Your profile displays:

- **Personal Information**
  - Full name
  - Email address
  - Phone number
  - Date of birth (optional)
  - Address

- **Account Settings**
  - Login email
  - Password settings
  - Notification preferences

- **Service History**
  - Total visits
  - Favorite services
  - Regular stylist(s)

### Updating Profile Information

1. Go to **"Profile"**
2. Click **"Edit"** or the pencil icon
3. Update your information:
   - Name
   - Phone number
   - Address
   - Date of birth
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **"Profile"**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter your **current password**
4. Enter your **new password** (twice for confirmation)
5. Click **"Update Password"**

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- At least one number
- At least one special character (!@#$%^&*)

### Notification Preferences

1. Go to **"Profile"** → **"Notifications"**
2. Toggle preferences for:
   - **Email notifications** - Appointment reminders, promotions
   - **SMS notifications** - Text message alerts
   - **Push notifications** - In-app notifications
   - **Promotional emails** - Marketing and offers

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### General Questions

**Q: How do I create an account?**
A: Click "Register" on the homepage, fill in your details, and follow the verification steps.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: No, one email address = one account. Use the same login each time.

**Q: How far in advance can I book?**
A: Up to 30 days in advance. Bookings must be at least 2 hours ahead.

**Q: What if my preferred stylist is not available?**
A: You can select "Any Stylist" to get faster availability, or check other dates/times for your preferred stylist.

### Appointment Questions

**Q: Can I reschedule an appointment?**
A: Yes, if it's at least 2 hours away. Go to "My Appointments" and click "Reschedule".

**Q: What's the cancellation policy?**
A: Cancel at least 2 hours in advance. Same-day cancellations may have penalties.

**Q: Will I be reminded about my appointment?**
A: Yes! You'll receive email and/or SMS reminders based on your preferences.

**Q: What if I'm late for my appointment?**
A: Contact your branch directly. Late arrivals may be rescheduled or charged a fee.

### Billing Questions

**Q: When will I be charged?**
A: Charges typically apply when the service is completed, not at booking.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
A: Cash, card, vouchers, and gift cards (depending on branch).

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refunds depend on your salon's refund policy. Contact the branch directly.

**Q: How do I dispute a charge?**
A: Contact your branch manager or the main office.

### Rewards Questions

**Q: How do I earn loyalty points?**
A: Earn points on every service purchase. Points accumulate automatically.

**Q: How do I check my points balance?**
A: Click "Rewards" from the main menu to see your current balance.

**Q: Can I transfer points to another person?**
A: No, loyalty points are personal to your account.

**Q: Do points expire?**
A: Check with your salon for their loyalty program terms.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page and follow the recovery process.

**Q: Why can't I see available times for my preferred stylist?**
A: That stylist may be fully booked. Try another date or select "Any Stylist".

**Q: The page is loading slowly.**
A: Try refreshing your browser or clearing your cache.

**Q: I'm getting an error message.**
A: Note the error message and contact your system administrator.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact your salon's System Administrator or manager.

**Q: Can I have multiple logins?**
A: No, each email address is one account. Use your main email.

**Q: How is my personal information protected?**
A: Your data is encrypted and stored securely using industry-standard security measures.
