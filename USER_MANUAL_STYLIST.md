## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the David's Salon login page
2. Enter your **email address** or **username**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **"Sign In"**

### Role Selection

If you have multiple roles:
1. After login, you may see a **"Select Your Role"** prompt
2. Click **"Stylist"** to access the stylist dashboard
3. Your dashboard will load with stylist-specific features

### First Login Tips

- Update your profile picture for client recognition
- Review your schedule for the week
- Check for any pending leave requests
- Set your notification preferences

## Viewing Your Appointments

### Today's Appointments

The dashboard shows your appointments for today:

- **Time** - Appointment start time
- **Client Name** - Who you're serving
- **Service** - What service you're providing
- **Duration** - How long the appointment takes
- **Notes** - Any special client requests
- **Status** - Current status (pending, confirmed, in-service, completed)

### Full Appointment List

1. Click **"My Appointments"** from the menu
2. View all your upcoming appointments
3. Appointments are sorted by date/time
4. Filter options:
   - **Today**
   - **This Week**
   - **Upcoming**
   - **All**

### Appointment Details

Click on any appointment to view:

- **Client Information**
  - Name, phone, email
  - Profile picture
  - Notes/preferences
  - Visit history

- **Service Details**
  - Service name and description
  - Duration and cost
  - Any add-on services
  - Special requests

- **Branch Information**
  - Location details
  - Address and parking
  - Branch hours

- **Status History**
  - When booked
  - Any rescheduling history
  - Current status

### Appointment Status

- **Pending** - Awaiting confirmation
- **Confirmed** - Scheduled and confirmed
- **In-Service** - Currently being served
- **Completed** - Service finished
- **Cancelled** - Cancelled appointment
- **No-Show** - Client did not arrive

## Managing Your Schedule

### Calendar View

1. Click **"My Schedule"** from the menu
2. View your schedule in calendar format
3. Toggle between **Month**, **Week**, and **Day** views
4. Click on any day to see details

### Weekly Schedule

**Week View Benefits:**
- See your entire week at a glance
- Identify busy periods
- Plan work-life balance
- Spot scheduling conflicts

**Week View Shows:**
- Time slots (e.g., 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM)
- Your appointments color-coded
- Available time slots (empty blocks)
- Break times

### Updating Your Availability

*Note: Typically managed by Branch Manager, but you may have self-service options*

1. Contact your **Branch Manager** to modify your schedule
2. Submit schedule changes via **"Schedule Request"** (if available)
3. Manager reviews and approves changes

### Handling Schedule Changes

**Rescheduling Requests:**
1. When a client requests a reschedule, you'll be notified
2. Review your availability
3. Accept or suggest alternative times
4. Manager finalizes the change

**Schedule Conflicts:**
- System prevents double-booking
- If a conflict occurs, contact your manager
- Your manager adjusts the schedule

### Time Off & Breaks

- **Lunch Breaks** - Built into your schedule (usually 1 hour)
- **Short Breaks** - Between appointments (usually 10-15 mins)
- **Personal Time** - Request via Leave Management
- **Holidays** - Branch calendar shows holidays (branch closed)

## Client Check-Ins

### Checking In a Client

When a client arrives for their appointment:

1. Click **"Check Ins"** from the menu
2. Find the appointment in the **"Pending Check-Ins"** list
3. Click **"Check In"** next to the client's name
4. Confirm the appointment details match
5. Click **"Confirm Check-In"**

The system will:
- Mark the appointment as checked in
- Update the appointment status
- Notify the client
- Start tracking service time

### Check-In Details

You can see:
- Client name and photo
- Service scheduled
- Appointment time
- Any notes or special requests
- Client contact information

### Managing No-Shows

If a client doesn't arrive:

1. When their appointment time passes, click **"Mark No-Show"**
2. Confirm the no-show
3. System records it in the client's history
4. Helps track attendance patterns

### Late Arrivals

For clients arriving late:

1. Check in the client anyway
2. Adjust the appointment duration if needed
3. Notify the manager if service needs to be shortened
4. Document the late arrival in notes

## Building Your Portfolio

Your portfolio showcases your work to potential clients.

### Accessing Your Portfolio

1. Click **"Portfolio"** from the menu
2. View your complete portfolio
3. See how clients will view your work

### Adding Portfolio Items

1. Go to **"Portfolio"**
2. Click **"Add New Work"** or **"Upload Photo"**
3. Select or upload an image of your work:
   - Before/after photos
   - Hairstyle examples
   - Hair coloring samples
   - Treatment results
4. Add details:
   - **Service Category** - Type of service
   - **Service Name** - Specific service
   - **Description** - Brief description of the work
   - **Date** - When you completed it
   - **Client Approval** - Confirm client consented to posting
5. Click **"Save"**

### Portfolio Best Practices

✅ **Do:**
- Use high-quality photos with good lighting
- Include before and after shots
- Get client permission before posting
- Highlight your specializations
- Update regularly with recent work
- Include diverse examples

❌ **Don't:**
- Post without client consent
- Use poor quality photos
- Include client identifying information (names, faces)
- Post outdated work only
- Neglect your portfolio

### Managing Portfolio Items

1. Go to **"Portfolio"**
2. Hover over any portfolio item
3. Options to:
   - **Edit** - Update description or details
   - **Delete** - Remove the item
   - **Feature** - Make it a featured/primary item

## Tracking Commissions

Your commission tracking helps you monitor your earnings.

### Commission Overview

1. Click **"Commission"** from the menu
2. View your commission dashboard showing:
   - **Current Month Commission** - This month's earnings
   - **Total YTD** - Year-to-date earnings
   - **Commission Breakdown** - By service or category
   - **Previous Months** - Historical data

### Commission Calculation

**Your commission is calculated based on:**
- Services completed
- Commissionable service types (varies by salon)
- Commission percentage (set by management)
- Billing/payment status

**NOT included:**
- Cancelled services
- No-show appointments
- Unpaid/pending transactions (usually)

### Viewing Commission Details

1. Click on any month or period
2. See detailed breakdown:
   - **Date** - Service date
   - **Client** - Client name
   - **Service** - What service was provided
   - **Amount** - Service cost
   - **Commission %** - Your commission percentage
   - **Commission Earned** - Your cut

### Commission Payment

**Payment Schedule:**
- Typically monthly (check with your manager)
- Paid on a specific date each month
- May include bonus structures

**Check Payment Details:**
1. Go to **"Commission"**
2. Click **"Payment History"**
3. View:
   - Payment dates
   - Amounts paid
   - Payment method
   - Status

### Commission Disputes

If you see an error in your commission:

1. Document the discrepancy
2. Contact your **Branch Manager**
3. Provide specific appointment/service details
4. Manager investigates and corrects if needed

## Leave Management

Request and track time off.

### Submitting a Leave Request

1. Click **"Leave Request"** or **"Leave Management"** from menu
2. Click **"Request Leave"** or **"New Request"**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Leave Type** - Vacation, sick leave, personal, other
   - **Start Date** - First day of leave
   - **End Date** - Last day of leave
   - **Duration** - Number of days
   - **Reason** - Optional explanation
4. Click **"Submit"**

### Leave Request Status

- **Pending** - Awaiting manager approval
- **Approved** - Accepted, you're off those dates
- **Rejected** - Denied, you're expected to work
- **Cancelled** - You cancelled the request

### Viewing Your Leave History

1. Go to **"Leave Management"**
2. View all leave requests (current and past)
3. See approval status for each

### Leave Approval Timeline

- Manager reviews requests
- Notification sent of approval/denial
- Approved leaves appear on your calendar
- Conflicts are communicated

### Leave Policies

*Check with your manager for specific policies:*

- Minimum notice required
- Maximum days per month
- Blackout dates
- Paid vs. unpaid leave

## Profile & Password Management

### Accessing Your Profile

1. Click on your **name/avatar** in top-right corner
2. Select **"Profile"** or **"My Account"**

### Profile Information

Your profile displays:

- **Personal Information**
  - Full name
  - Email address
  - Phone number
  - Date of birth

- **Employment Details**
  - Role (Stylist)
  - Branch assignment
  - Hire date
  - Specializations/certifications

- **Professional Details**
  - License number (if applicable)
  - Certifications
  - Languages spoken
  - Service specialties

### Updating Your Profile

1. Go to **"Profile"**
2. Click **"Edit"** or pencil icon
3. Update:
   - Profile picture
   - Contact information
   - Professional details
   - Service specializations
4. Click **"Save"**

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **"Profile"**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter **current password**
4. Enter **new password** (twice for confirmation)
5. Click **"Update"**

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of uppercase/lowercase
- At least one number
- At least one special character

### Setting Up Role PIN (if required)

Some salons use PIN access for role switching:

1. Go to **"Profile"**
2. Click **"Set PIN"** or **"Role PIN"**
3. Enter a 4-6 digit PIN
4. Confirm the PIN
5. Save

This PIN may be required when switching to the stylist role.

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### General Questions

**Q: How do I see my full schedule?**
A: Click "My Schedule" to see your calendar, or "My Appointments" for a list view.

**Q: Can I change my schedule?**
A: Contact your Branch Manager to request schedule changes.

**Q: How often is commission calculated?**
A: Usually monthly. Check with your manager for exact dates.

**Q: Who has access to my portfolio?**
A: Your portfolio is visible to potential clients on the public website.

### Appointment Questions

**Q: What should I do if a client is late?**
A: Check them in anyway and note the late arrival. Inform your manager if service needs adjustment.

**Q: What if I need to reschedule a client?**
A: Coordinate with your manager or the receptionist. Clients can also reschedule themselves through the portal.

**Q: How do I handle client cancellations?**
A: The system updates when a client cancels. You'll be notified of the cancellation.

**Q: What notes should I add after a service?**
A: Include what you did, results, client feedback, and any recommendations for next visit.

### Commission Questions

**Q: Why is my commission lower than expected?**
A: Some services may not be commissionable. Check with your manager about commission rules.

**Q: When will I be paid?**
A: Usually on a set date monthly. See your manager for specific payment schedules.

**Q: How do I dispute a commission amount?**
A: Document the issue and contact your Branch Manager.

**Q: Are cancelled services included in commission?**
A: No, only completed, paid services typically count.

### Technical Issues

**Q: I can't see today's appointments?**
A: Try refreshing the page. If persists, contact your manager.

**Q: Why can't I update my portfolio?**
A: Check image file size (should be under 5MB). Try a different file format.

**Q: The schedule isn't updating.**
A: Ask your manager to verify your schedule in the system.

**Q: I'm not receiving appointment reminders.**
A: Check your notification preferences in Profile settings.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on login, or go to Profile and click "Change Password".

**Q: Can I have multiple logins?**
A: No, one email = one account. Contact your manager if you need access restored.

**Q: How is my data protected?**
A: All data is encrypted and stored securely with industry-standard security.
