## Stock Alerts & Monitoring

### Organization-Wide Low Stock Alerts

1. Click **"Stock Alerts"** from the menu
2. View all low stock items across organization
3. See:
   - Product name
   - Which branches are low
   - Current quantity
   - Reorder point
   - Supplier

### Alert Prioritization

Alerts are prioritized by:

- **Critical** - Out of stock
- **High** - Below minimum threshold
- **Medium** - Approaching reorder point
- **Low** - Just something to watch

### Responding to Alerts

1. Review alert
2. Check current quantity across branches
3. Determine if:
   - Need to order more
   - Can transfer between branches
   - Should adjust reorder point
4. Take action:
   - Create purchase order
   - Initiate transfer
   - Investigate if slow-moving

### Stock Alert Thresholds

Default reorder points may need adjustment:

1. Review slow-moving products - raise threshold to reduce overstock
2. Review fast-moving products - lower threshold to prevent stockouts
3. Seasonal items - adjust based on season
4. New products - monitor closely to set appropriate levels

### Critical Stock Situations

Handle out-of-stock of critical items:

1. Contact supplier for emergency delivery
2. Check all branches for available stock
3. Initiate emergency transfer if needed
4. Communicate with branch managers
5. Prioritize getting stock quickly

## Expiry Tracking

### Organization Expiry View

1. Click **"Expiry Tracker"** from the menu
2. View all products with expiration dates across organization
3. Organized by expiration date (soonest first)
4. Color-coded by urgency

### Expiring by Branch

Drill down to see expiring items at each branch:

1. Go to **"Expiry Tracker"**
2. View expiring products:
   - Which branches have them
   - Quantity at each branch
   - Days until expiration
   - Value at risk

### Expiry Alerts

System alerts you to products expiring:

- **Within 7 days** - Red (urgent action needed)
- **Within 30 days** - Yellow (plan usage)
- **Beyond 30 days** - Green (monitor)

### Managing Expiring Inventory

Strategy for products about to expire:

1. **Use in Services** - Include in treatments
2. **Promote to Clients** - Discount products about to expire
3. **Staff Discounts** - Let staff buy at cost
4. **Transfer Branches** - Move to branch that uses more
5. **Dispose** - Remove when time expires

### Preventing Expiry Issues

Best practices:

- **First In, First Out (FIFO)** - Use older stock first
- **Regular Monitoring** - Check weekly
- **Proper Storage** - Maintain conditions
- **Clear Expiration Dates** - Make them visible
- **Training** - Educate staff on shelf life
- **Ordering** - Request longer shelf life from suppliers

## Stock Optimization

### Inventory Level Optimization

Determine optimal stock levels:

1. **Analyze Sales Data** - How fast items sell
2. **Check Lead Times** - How long to receive replacements
3. **Set Reorder Points** - When to reorder
4. **Set Order Quantities** - How much to order each time

**Formula:**
- Reorder Point = (Daily Sales × Lead Time in Days) + Safety Stock
- Order Quantity = (Reorder Point + Daily Sales × 30 days)

### Right-Sizing Inventory by Branch

1. Compare inventory levels across branches
2. Identify overstock situations (excess tied-up capital)
3. Identify understock situations (stockout risk)
4. Rebalance through:
   - Transfers between branches
   - Adjusted reorder points
   - Sales promotions for overstock
   - Orders to prevent understock

### Reducing Overstock

Free up capital tied in excess inventory:

1. Identify overstocked items
2. **Strategies:**
   - Increase usage in services
   - Create bundle promotions
   - Offer staff discounts
   - Transfer to branches that use more
   - Donate excess
3. Monitor and adjust ordering

### Improving Inventory Turnover

Faster turnover improves cash flow:

1. Reduce lead times through faster suppliers
2. Improve demand forecasting
3. Adjust order quantities
4. Promote slow-moving items
5. Discontinue never-selling items
6. Align ordering with sales patterns

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Inventory Management

**Q: How do I balance inventory across branches?**
A: Use inventory data to identify overstock/understock, then coordinate transfers or adjust ordering.

**Q: What's the best reorder point?**
A: (Daily Usage × Lead Time) + Safety Stock. Adjust based on actual experience.

**Q: How often should I audit inventory?**
A: Full audit quarterly or annually. Cycle audits monthly by category.

**Q: Why does one branch have so much more inventory?**
A: May reflect branch size, service mix, or ordering patterns. Review with branch manager.

### Purchasing

**Q: Should I consolidate to fewer suppliers?**
A: Review supplier performance. Top performers may offer better pricing for volume.

**Q: How can I reduce purchasing costs?**
A: Consolidate orders, negotiate volume discounts, improve forecasting, reduce overstock.

**Q: What lead time should I plan for?**
A: Varies by supplier. Track actual lead times, include buffer time for safety.

### Expiry & Disposal

**Q: How do I prevent products from expiring?**
A: Use FIFO method, monitor expiry tracker weekly, use older stock first, order with longer shelf life.

**Q: What should I do with products about to expire?**
A: Use in services, promote to clients, offer staff discounts, transfer between branches.

**Q: Is there a disposal cost?**
A: Depends on product type. Some can be donated; others require proper disposal. Check regulations.

### Technical

**Q: Why is the dashboard loading slowly?**
A: May be due to data volume. Try filtering to specific branches or products.

**Q: The report won't generate.**
A: Check date range is valid. Try filtering to fewer branches or products.

**Q: Inventory numbers seem wrong.**
A: Run audits to verify physical counts. Review adjustments and receipts for errors.
