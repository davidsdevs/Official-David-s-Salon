## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the salon management system login page
2. Enter your **email address** or **username**
3. Enter your **password**
4. Click **"Sign In"**

### Role Selection

If you have multiple roles:
1. After login, select **"Receptionist"** from the role selection screen
2. The receptionist dashboard will load
3. You can switch roles by clicking your profile menu

### Initial Setup

- Familiarize yourself with the dashboard layout
- Learn the main menu navigation
- Set your notification preferences

## Managing Appointments

### Creating a New Appointment

**Method 1: Quick Book (from Dashboard)**
1. Click **"Quick Book"** or **"New Appointment"** button
2. Select the **Branch** (if applicable)
3. Choose the **Client** - Search existing or create new
4. Select **Service** category and specific service
5. Choose preferred **Stylist** or leave as "Any Stylist"
6. Select **Date** from the calendar
7. Select **Time Slot** from available times
8. Add any **Notes** or special requests
9. Click **"Confirm Booking"**

**Method 2: Full Appointment Page**
1. Click **"Appointments"** from the menu
2. Click **"New Appointment"** or **"Book Appointment"**
3. Fill in all appointment details
4. Click **"Save"**

### Searching for an Appointment

1. Go to **"Appointments"**
2. Use the **search bar** at the top
3. Search by:
   - Client name
   - Appointment date
   - Stylist name
   - Status
4. Results display instantly

### Viewing Appointment Details

Click on any appointment to see:

- **Client Information** - Name, phone, email
- **Service Details** - Service, duration, price
- **Stylist Information** - Assigned stylist
- **Status** - Current appointment status
- **Notes** - Special requests or alerts
- **History** - Previous services with this stylist

### Rescheduling an Appointment

1. Find the appointment in the list
2. Click **"Reschedule"** or the reschedule icon
3. Select a new **date** and **time**
4. Confirm availability with the stylist (system validates)
5. Click **"Save Changes"**
6. Client receives notification of the change

### Cancelling an Appointment

1. Find the appointment
2. Click **"Cancel"** or the trash icon
3. Confirm the cancellation
4. The system will:
   - Update the appointment status
   - Free up the stylist's time
   - Notify the client
   - Remove from the schedule

### Confirming Appointments

Some appointments start as "Pending":

1. Review the pending appointment
2. Verify stylist availability
3. Click **"Confirm"**
4. Client receives confirmation notification

### Managing Appointment Conflicts

The system prevents double-booking:
- Same stylist can't have overlapping appointments
- System shows "Time Unavailable" if there's a conflict
- Alternative times are suggested

### Walk-In Appointments

For walk-in clients:

1. Click **"Walk-In Appointment"** or **"Quick Book"**
2. Select the client (may need to create profile first)
3. Choose available stylist and time slot
4. Note in comments: "Walk-in"
5. Immediately proceed to check-in

## Client Check-Ins & Arrivals

### Check-In Process

When a client arrives:

1. Click **"Arrivals"** from the menu or dashboard
2. Find the client in the **"Pending Check-Ins"** list
3. Click **"Check In"** next to their name
4. Verify appointment details:
   - Client name
   - Service
   - Stylist
   - Time
5. Click **"Confirm Check-In"**

System updates:
- Appointment status changes to "In-Service"
- Service timer starts
- Stylist is notified
- Client is confirmed as present

### Arrivals Dashboard

The **"Arrivals"** page shows:

- **Pending Check-Ins** - Clients who haven't arrived
- **Checked In** - Clients currently being served
- **No-Shows** - Clients who didn't appear
- Time since appointment started

### Handling Late Arrivals

If a client arrives late:

1. Check them in as normal (system adjusts timing)
2. Inform the stylist of the late arrival
3. Assess if service can still be completed
4. Adjust appointment duration if needed
5. Note in the appointment: "Arrived [X] minutes late"

### No-Show Management

For a client who doesn't arrive:

1. Wait the appropriate grace period (check salon policy)
2. Click **"Mark as No-Show"**
3. Confirm the no-show status
4. System records:
   - No-show in client history
   - Freeing up stylist's time for reschedule
   - Alert for manager review

### Walk-In Check-In

Walk-ins checking in:

1. Create appointment first (see Walk-In Appointments)
2. Immediately proceed to check-in
3. Mark as checked in
4. Route to available stylist

## Billing & POS

### Creating a Bill

**Method 1: Automatic (Post-Service)**
1. After appointment completes, click **"Process Payment"**
2. System pulls appointment details
3. Service shows automatically
4. Add any additional items (products, services)
5. Apply discounts/promotions if eligible
6. Proceed to payment

**Method 2: Manual Billing**
1. Click **"Billing"** from the menu
2. Click **"New Bill"** or **"Create Transaction"**
3. Select the **Client**
4. Add **Line Items**:
   - Services provided
   - Products sold
5. Apply **Discounts** or **Promotions**
6. Proceed to payment

### Adding Line Items to Bills

**Services:**
1. Click **"Add Service"**
2. Select service from dropdown
3. Service price auto-populates
4. Quantity (usually 1)
5. Add notes if needed

**Products:**
1. Click **"Add Product"**
2. Search for product
3. Select quantity
4. Price auto-populates
5. Add notes if needed

### Applying Discounts & Promotions

1. In the bill details, find **"Discounts/Promotions"** section
2. Click **"Apply Discount"**
3. Options:
   - **Percentage Off** - Enter discount percentage
   - **Fixed Amount** - Enter fixed discount
   - **Loyalty Redemption** - Apply loyalty points
   - **Promotion Code** - Enter promo code
4. System calculates new total
5. Add discount reason in notes

### Tax Calculation

Tax is automatically calculated based on:
- Items in the bill
- Tax configuration set by management
- Tax category of each item

### Processing Payments

1. After all items added, review the **Bill Summary**:
   - Subtotal
   - Discounts
   - Taxes
   - **Total Amount Due**

2. Click **"Process Payment"**

3. Select **Payment Method**:
   - **Cash** - Exact change or provide change amount
   - **Card** - Process card payment (if integrated)
   - **Voucher** - Enter voucher code
   - **Gift Card** - Scan or enter gift card number
   - **Multiple Payment** - Combine methods

4. Enter payment details for selected method

5. Click **"Complete Payment"**

6. Receipt prints (or save/email receipt)

### Voiding a Transaction

If a transaction is made in error:

1. Find the transaction in **"Billing"** or **"Transactions"**
2. Click **"Void"**
3. Enter reason for voiding
4. Confirm the void
5. Transaction marked as voided (not deleted)
6. Amount can be refunded

### Refunding a Transaction

For returns or issues:

1. Locate the transaction
2. Click **"Refund"**
3. Enter:
   - **Refund Amount** (full or partial)
   - **Reason for Refund**
4. Select **Refund Method**:
   - Original payment method
   - Store credit
   - Cash
5. Confirm and process refund

### Loyalty Points

When processing payments:

1. Clients earn loyalty points automatically
2. Points calculated on service total (before discount)
3. Points display on receipt
4. Client can view points in their profile
5. Points can be redeemed for rewards

### Gift Cards

If your salon offers gift cards:

1. Click **"Sell Gift Card"** from billing menu
2. Enter **Gift Card Amount**
3. Client pays for the card
4. Card number generated
5. Client receives card details
6. System tracks card balance

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Appointment Management

**Q: How do I book an appointment for a walk-in?**
A: Click "Quick Book" on the dashboard, select the client, service, and available stylist/time. Mark as walk-in in notes.

**Q: Can I book appointments after hours?**
A: Yes, the system allows booking for future dates. Same-day bookings may have time restrictions.

**Q: What if a stylist calls in sick?**
A: Contact your manager to update the schedule. You may need to reschedule affected clients.

**Q: How do I reschedule a client's appointment?**
A: Find the appointment, click "Reschedule", select new date/time, and confirm.

### Billing Questions

**Q: Why won't the system accept a card payment?**
A: Card processing may not be integrated. Use cash, voucher, or gift card instead.

**Q: How do I apply a loyalty discount?**
A: During payment, click "Apply Promotion" and select the loyalty redemption option.

**Q: Can I issue a refund after closing?**
A: Yes, you can process refunds anytime. Log in and find the transaction to refund.

**Q: How do I void a transaction?**
A: Find the transaction, click "Void", enter reason, and confirm. Transaction marked as voided.

### Client Management

**Q: How do I find a client's previous services?**
A: Open their profile and click "Service History" to see all past appointments.

**Q: Can I add notes about a client?**
A: Yes, open their profile and add notes in the Notes section.

**Q: What if I create a duplicate client profile?**
A: Contact your manager to merge duplicate profiles.

### Sales & Reporting

**Q: Why is my daily total different than expected?**
A: Check that all voided/refunded transactions are excluded. Review filtered results.

**Q: Can I print sales reports?**
A: Yes, export the report as PDF and print from your browser.

**Q: How often should I reconcile sales?**
A: Typically at end of shift. Check your salon's procedures.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The page isn't loading properly.**
A: Try refreshing the browser or clearing cache.

**Q: I can't find a client in the search.**
A: Try searching by phone number instead of name.

**Q: An appointment won't save.**
A: Verify all required fields are filled. Contact IT if error persists.

**Q: I'm getting an error when processing payment.**
A: Note the error message and contact your manager or IT.
