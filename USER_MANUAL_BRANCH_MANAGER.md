## Staff Management

1. Click **"Staff Management"** from the menu
2. View all staff members at your branch
3. Staff listed with current status:
   - **Active** - Currently employed
   - **Inactive** - On leave or terminated

### Staff Information

Each staff member shows:

- **Name & Photo** - Identification
- **Role** - Position (stylist, receptionist, etc.)
- **Employment Status** - Active/inactive
- **Contact Information** - Phone, email
- **Hire Date** - Start date
- **Specializations** - Areas of expertise
- **Services Offered** - What they provide

### Adding Staff

1. Click **"Add Staff"** or **"New Staff Member"**
2. Fill in personal information:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number
   - Date of birth
   - Address
3. Set employment details:
   - **Role** - Position in salon
   - **Hire Date** - Start date
   - **Contract Type** - Full-time, part-time

4. Add professional details:
   - **Certifications** - License numbers, qualifications
   - **Specializations** - Areas of expertise
   - **Services** - What services they offer

### Editing Staff Information

1. Find the staff member
2. Click **"Edit"** or the pencil icon
3. Update relevant information
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Deactivating/Terminating Staff

1. Open staff member's profile
2. Click **"Deactivate"** or **"Terminate"**
3. Confirm the action
4. Staff no longer appears in active roster
5. Historical data is preserved

Track staff qualifications:

## Calendar Management

### Branch Calendar

The branch calendar shows:

- **Public Holidays** - National holidays
- **Branch Closures** - Days the branch is closed
- **Special Events** - Promotions, special hours
- **Staff Availability** - When staff are scheduled

### Viewing the Calendar

1. Click **"Calendar Management"** from the menu
2. View calendar in **Month**, **Week**, or **Day** view
3. Color-coded events for easy identification

### Setting Branch Closures

For holidays or maintenance closures:

1. Go to **"Calendar Management"**
2. Click **"Add Event"** or the date
3. Select **"Branch Closure"**
4. Enter:
   - **Date** - Closure date
   - **Reason** - Why closed (holiday, maintenance, etc.)
   - **Full Day** - Toggle if full day or partial
   - **Time Range** - If partial closure
5. Save the closure

**Effects:**
- No appointments can be booked on closure dates
- Staff schedules auto-adjust
- Clients are notified of closure

### Setting Operating Hours

Configure your branch's operating hours:

1. Go to **"Branch Settings"** → **"Operating Hours"**
2. For each day of the week:
   - Set opening time
   - Set closing time
   - Toggle if branch is open
3. Save changes

**System Effects:**
- Appointment booking restricted to operating hours
- Availability shown only during open hours
- Staff schedules align with operating hours

### Special Hours/Events

For special occasions:

1. Go to **"Calendar Management"**
2. Click **"Add Special Event"**
3. Enter:
   - **Event Name** - (e.g., "Grand Opening")
   - **Date & Time** - When event occurs
   - **Special Hours** - Operating hours for event
   - **Notes** - Additional details
4. Save

## Deposits Management

### Understanding Deposits

Deposits are bank deposits made from daily revenue:

1. Cash and payments collected from clients
2. Counted and prepared for bank deposit
3. Tracked in the system
4. Reconciled with actual bank deposits

### Recording Daily Deposits

1. Click **"Deposits"** from the menu
2. Click **"New Deposit"** or **"Record Deposit"**
3. Enter:
   - **Deposit Date** - When deposit was made
   - **Amount** - Total deposit amount
   - **Payment Methods Included** - Breakdown:
     - Cash: $X
     - Credit Card: $X
     - Vouchers: $X
     - Gift Cards: $X
   - **Notes** - Any discrepancies or notes
4. Click **"Save"**

### Reconciling Deposits

Match system deposits with actual bank deposits:

1. Go to **"Deposits"**
2. Click **"Reconcile"** next to a deposit
3. Enter **actual bank deposit amount**
4. System compares to recorded amount
5. If match: Click **"Confirm"**
6. If mismatch: Investigate discrepancy

### Deposit Discrepancies

If recorded and actual deposits don't match:

1. Review the deposit details
2. Check all transactions for that period
3. Verify cash count accuracy
4. Look for missing transactions
5. Document the discrepancy
6. Contact operational manager if needed

### Multiple Deposits Per Day

Your branch may make multiple deposits per day:

1. Each deposit is recorded separately
2. Each can be reconciled independently
3. Daily summary shows total deposited
4. Helpful for branches with high volume

## Inventory Management

### Inventory Dashboard

1. Click **"Inventory"** from the menu
2. View inventory overview:
   - **Total Products** - Number of items
   - **Low Stock Items** - Items below threshold
   - **Out of Stock** - Items with zero quantity
   - **Stock Value** - Total inventory value

### Managing Stock

**Viewing Stock:**
1. Go to **"Inventory"**
2. Browse all products with current quantities
3. See status: In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock

**Adjusting Stock:**
1. Find the product
2. Click **"Adjust Stock"**
3. Enter:
   - **Adjustment Type** - Add or Remove
   - **Quantity** - Amount to adjust
   - **Reason** - Why adjusting (damage, usage, recount, etc.)
4. Save

**Stock History:**
- Each adjustment is recorded
- View complete history of stock changes
- Track adjustments and dates

### Purchase Orders

Order stock from suppliers:

1. Click **"Purchase Orders"** from menu
2. Click **"New Purchase Order"**
3. Select **Supplier**
4. Add products:
   - Click **"Add Item"**
   - Select product
   - Enter quantity
   - Confirm unit price
5. Add shipping/fees if applicable
6. Review total cost
7. Click **"Submit"**

**Purchase Order Status:**
- **Pending** - Not yet sent to supplier
- **Ordered** - Sent to supplier
- **Shipped** - In transit
- **Delivered** - Received at branch

### Deliveries

Track incoming stock:

1. Click **"Deliveries"** from menu
2. View expected and received deliveries
3. When delivery arrives:
   - Click **"Receive Delivery"**
   - Verify items match order
   - Count items to confirm quantities
   - Click **"Confirm Receipt"**
4. Stock automatically updates

### Low Stock Alerts

The system alerts you to low stock:

1. Alerts appear on dashboard
2. Go to **"Inventory"** to review
3. Products below threshold are highlighted
4. Create purchase order to restock

### Expiry Tracking

For products with expiration dates:

1. Go to **"Inventory"** or **"Expiry Tracker"**
2. View products by expiration date
3. Items expiring soon are highlighted
4. Remove expired products from inventory
5. System tracks removed items

## Commissions & Payments

- Service amount (before discount)
- Payment status (only completed/paid services)

## FAQ & Troubleshooting

### Staff Management

**Q: How do I add a new stylist?**
A: Go to Staff Management, click "Add Staff", fill in their information, and save.

**Q: Can I change a stylist's role?**
A: Yes, edit their staff profile and update the role.

**Q: How do I track staff certifications?**
A: Open their profile, go to Certifications, and add their licenses/credentials.

### Appointments

**Q: What if two stylists call in sick?**
A: Contact affected clients to reschedule. Update staff schedule and contact operational manager if coverage needed.

**Q: How do I handle an appointment conflict?**
A: The system prevents double-booking. If a conflict occurs, reschedule one appointment.

**Q: Can clients reschedule themselves?**
A: Yes, through the client portal, or you can reschedule on their behalf.

### Financial

**Q: Why doesn't a transaction show in my sales report?**
A: Check the transaction status. Voided or refunded transactions may be excluded.

**Q: How do I reconcile a deposit discrepancy?**
A: Review all transactions for that period, check for missing entries, verify cash count.

**Q: How is commission calculated?**
A: Commission = Service Amount × Commission Percentage. Only completed, paid services count.

### Inventory

**Q: What's the minimum stock level?**
A: Set by your operational manager. Reorder when stock reaches this level.

**Q: How do I track product expiration?**
A: Go to "Expiry Tracker" to see items by expiration date.

**Q: Can I adjust stock for waste/damage?**
A: Yes, go to Inventory and click "Adjust Stock", then select "Damage" as reason.

### Services

**Q: Can I set different prices at my branch?**
A: Yes, use "Branch Pricing" to override master service prices.

**Q: How do I add a new service?**
A: Go to Services Management, click "Add New Service", and fill in details.

**Q: Can stylists only perform certain services?**
A: Yes, in Services Management, select which stylists can perform each service.

### Technical Issues

**Q: The page is loading slowly.**
A: Try refreshing the browser or clearing cache.

**Q: I can't find a specific report.**
A: Check the Reports menu. If still missing, contact IT or operational manager.

**Q: A transaction didn't save.**
A: Verify all required fields are filled. Try again or contact IT.
